<div class="kicker">01 · EXPERT CRITERIA</div>

# Criteria carve out a useful search space.

<div class="criteria-flow">
  <div class="criteria-broad">
    <span>VAGUE TASK</span>
    <b>“Tell me a climate story.”</b>
    <small>Almost anything plausible could fit.</small>
  </div>
  <div class="criteria-arrow">→</div>
  <div class="criteria-dimensions">
    <span>Hong Kong</span>
    <span>2100</span>
    <span>one affected person</span>
    <span>one checked statistic</span>
    <span>90 seconds</span>
    <span>name the uncertainty</span>
  </div>
  <div class="criteria-arrow">→</div>
  <div class="criteria-fit">
    <span>CONDITIONED TASK</span>
    <b>Smaller.<br>Relevant.<br>Testable.</b>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="criteria-principle"><b>Expertise is not “more words.”</b> It is knowing which dimensions determine fitness.</div>

<div class="source">Teaching model: prompts condition token probabilities; they do not literally cut a fixed geometric space. Brown et al. (2020) [2]; Hugging Face [21].</div>

<div class="kicker">03 · THE MOVING FRONTIER</div>

# The model can help improve the brief too.

<div class="refine-loop">
  <div><b>1</b><strong>Read the task</strong><span>Interpret the provisional goal.</span></div>
  <div><b>2</b><strong>Expose gaps</strong><span>Suggest missing criteria and edge cases.</span></div>
  <div><b>3</b><strong>Rewrite the brief</strong><span>Generate clearer instructions or rubrics.</span></div>
  <div><b>4</b><strong>Compare outputs</strong><span>Use feedback to propose another iteration.</span></div>
</div>

<div class="refine-turn">Prompt-writing is a shrinking moat.</div>

<div class="source">LLMs can participate in iterative prompt optimisation when candidate prompts receive an evaluation signal. Yang et al. (2024) [20]. Performance depends on the model, task and evaluator.</div>

<div class="kicker">04 · QUALITY ASSURANCE</div>

# A model can check.<br>An expert must verify.

<div class="qa-grid">
  <div class="qa-assist">
    <span>AI-ASSISTED CHECKING</span>
    <b>Fast, broad, repeatable</b>
    <ul>
      <li>Compare against a rubric</li>
      <li>Flag omissions and inconsistencies</li>
      <li>Generate counterarguments</li>
      <li>Test alternative wording</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="qa-gate">
    <span>INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION</span>
    <b>Grounded, consequential, accountable</b>
    <ul>
      <li>Check the original evidence</li>
      <li>Recognise local and tacit context</li>
      <li>Judge acceptable trade-offs</li>
      <li>Own the decision if it fails</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="qa-warning">A model reviewing model-generated work is useful—but it is not automatically independent ground truth.</div>

<div class="source">In reasoning tasks studied by Huang et al. (2024), intrinsic self-correction without external feedback often failed to improve answers and could degrade them [24]. This does not mean AI checking never works.</div>
