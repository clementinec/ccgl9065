From Awareness to Action: Why 
Sustainable Choices Must Be the Better 
Choice 
By: Daniel Poon (3036518912) 
 

Climate change is a problem everyone knows about , but most are unwilling to do 
anything about it . This clear gap between what people support and what people do is exhibited in 
energy consumption . Meaningful changes in energy consumption remain limited as fossil fuels 
are still the dominant source of global energy consumption (International Energy Agency [IEA], 
2023). This pattern can be seen in Hong Kong specifically transport, in which transportation 
remains a major source of carbon emissions (Environmental Protection Department [EPD], 
2023). These evidence s reveal how people’s awareness of an issue does not a lways mean 
changes in behavior. This phenomenon is referred to as the “intention –action gap,” where 
individuals act differently to their value towards the environment (Kol lmuss & Agyeman, 2002). 
This gap exists because sustainable choices conflict with everyday convenience. 
The solution for this gap is simple . Sustainable choices should not collide with 
convenience. Instead of convinc ing people to walk the extra mile or downgrade to using public 
transport when they would have been driving a car, reducing fossil fuel usage should be done in 
a way that does not require sacrificing convenience. People would still choose options that are 
better, cheaper, and more practical, even when they support environmental goals. Especially in 
cities like Hong Kong, the fast -paced lifestyle here make convenience an important consideration 
in decision making. Since forcing people into impractical behavio rs is ineffective, the focus 
should be on developing clean technologies that people will prefer over fossil fuels . The goal 
should be to make sustainable choices, not necessarily the right choice, but the better choice. 
A example of successful energy transition is in China. Rather than relying on individuals 
to sacrifice convenience, the Chinese government has taken action to support the electric vehicle 
industry through subsidies and industrial policy (State Council of the People’s Republic of 
China, 2020). Within China’s national New Energy Vehicle Industry Development Plan (2021 –

2035) , long -term targets for EV development and adoption were established to accelerate 
industry growth (State Council of the People’s Republic of China, 2020). As a result, EVs have 
become more competitive with traditional petrol vehicles in both price and performance due to 
such large -scale investment (International Energy Agency, 2024). This strategy has significantly 
changed consumer behavior. In 2024, EVs accounted for 40.9% of all vehicle sales in China 
(China Association of Automobile Manufacturers [CAAM], 2025). Moreover, in July 2024, the 
monthly retail sales of EVs in China reached 51.1% of the market, surpassing traditional petrol 
and diesel cars for the first time (Xinhua News Agency, 2024). By actively supporting emerging 
clean energy technology, China has proven how a large scale transition towards clean energy can 
happen by making the clean energy technology better than their alternative. 
The success of Chinese EVs also have led to positive spillover effects within other 
places . In Hong Kong, Chinese EV sales have increased . These imported EVs are not only 
competitive in pricing, but they also come with advanced features only seen on high end petrol 
vehicles. This makes EVs in Hong Kong more attractive , and in 2024, electric vehicles 
accounted for over 70% of newly registered private cars in Hong Kong (Transport Department, 
2024). By improving the desirability of clean technology for people , behavioral change s will 
naturally happen without requiring individuals to sacrifice their everyday convenience. 
This solution, where the focus is on making sustainable options the better option can also 
lead to positive climate results in different sectors outside of transportation . In the energy sector, 
improvements in renewable energy such as solar panels have led to the prices of clean energy 
being cheaper . As a result, clean energy is now more competitive with fossil fuels (International 
Energy Agency, 2023) . This show how innovation is the key to large scale behavioral change . 

Rather than relying on individual sacrifice, climate action should make sustainable options an 
upgrade , just like how people w ould prefer solar energy if it was cheaper than its alternative. 
To summarize, the solution for clean energy transition should be eliminating the 
difference between what people know and do by making the sustainable options the better 
option. Instead of asking people to downgrad e their current lifestyle, the sustainable option 
should be focusing on bettering their current lifestyle . I firmly believe g overnments must 
increase their s upport for growing clean technologies, so firms can innovate and scale to become 
the most desirable options for everyday consumers. As individuals, the next step is simple , which 
is to start choosing the better option when it becomes available.
