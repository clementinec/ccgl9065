Urbanization Is Invading Our Earth

Environmental Education--Is it right?

I have been educated as an environmentally conscious person ever since I was in primary school. Ten years pass, I can still recall the mast landfill pictures that our teacher shown in order to brief us on how the impacts of climate change is growing. Having a deep impression on this, I have tried my best to replicate the instructions given by our teacher and mainstreamed media to fight against the worsen of the climate situations. Simple habits like using canvas or cotton bangs instead of plastic ones and use vacuum cup to avoid the consumption of deposit one have ingrained into my life. I have to admit that I am proud of my participation and sincerely hope that more people can take action and join the campaign.

This September, I walked into the classroom and learned a boarder picture of climate change. The professor elaborated on the more aspects that may cause climate change, from food consumption to energy transport. “The food consumption concealed a deeper problem that is beyond our impression.” As the professor’s speaking, I recognized that the link between food consumption and the intensification of climate change is tight. For example, Hong Kong import 90% of the food, the pollution emitted during food consumption, the Carbon Dioxide and the Nitric oxide etc., should undoubtedly claim for climate change. Apart from this, the heat island effect generating by the urbanization, the tons of dye water in fast fashion industry, the dominance of traditional method of power generation....My perspective of climate change gradually change. It is not just about simple plastic bags and disposable bottles, every shirt I wear, every piece of food in my stomach, every bus ride....Human life itself seems to be the “culprit” of climate change.

After class, I walked into a restaurant for lunch. Staring at the steak on my plate, the professor’s voice was still resounding. “How much carbon dioxide will be given out in just this meal?”, “why did I didn’t realize that simply eating, is worsening the situation of climate change?”, “Did anyone remind me before?”... The education that I received in the early age never stress the ubiquity of the factor that intensity climate change. Therefore, only now do I realize how pervasive climate change has become, already permeated into our life, which is a wide-ranging and urgent issue that we cannot tackle only by controlling the use of plastic product. But most of the kids received the same education, holding an incomplete and shallow perspective to climate change, forming awareness that is inaccurate.

Therefore, regarding this topic, I cannot help but question the effectiveness of the environmental education I received throughout my compulsory school years. Starting from scratch to let students form the habit of bringing own bags and bottles are undoubtedly worthwhile, but these habit only present the tip of iceberg. The education system presents climate as a problem that can be solved through individual and surface-level actions, but ignore that the problem itself is driven by systematic factors, and the deeper solution is absent from the curriculum. This education created a false sense of security: when I am not carrying plastic bags or using disposable bottle, I am doing my part. But the truth is, the students do not understand the complexity of the issue, and their effort is like clearing the ocean with a spoon.

This realization leads me to a clear conclusion, which is also the answer the question of how to move people, especially children to take action: government and educational institutions must expand and deepen the education on climate change. It should stop only teaching children how to do simple things like turning off the light and saving water as well as the reducing the use of plastic products. It must involve a comprehensive curriculum that explain the inner connection between the human activity and climate change. Through tracing a cotton shirt from farm to factory to home and calculating the carbon cost of a steak, the children will realize that “we should do more to protect the environment.

Some may think that this will let more people to support the climate instead of the development. But environmental education should not be confined to science or geography, it is a common topic for economics and sociology. Through which, they will expose to a boarder notion, examing whether people should support development or climate in different issue, helping them to develop the literacy to clarify their own stance. Through the cross-disciplinary involvement can help students build up their own viewpoint, enabling them to take wise action when facing climate-change related problem in the future.

To conclude, this course has given me inspiration on the deficiency of the whole picture in the environmental education. It is urgent that government and related institution revising the syllabus and build up children’s discernment.
